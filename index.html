Samuel El Hindy is a Lebanese entrepreneur and the founder of Colart Digital Marketing Agency. His career has moved through technology, education, management, sales, hospitality and live entertainment, and he has never let one job title define him.

Today he leads Colart's services, products, clients and creative direction, while still hosting nights behind the microphone.

Keep learning, keep building, and turn experience into something valuable.

A career that never followed a straight line

From robotics classrooms to management offices, from sales calls to the bar, and from an idea in 2019 to leading his own agency. Each chapter added something the next one needed.

  1. 2017to 2020

    Educational Assistant

    3Poli Robotics

    His first professional environment: supporting robotics sessions, workshops and student projects. It taught him to communicate, stay patient and explain complex ideas simply. It also gave him a way of thinking he still uses.

    1. Understand the objective
    2. Identify the parts
    3. Find the problem
    4. Build
    5. Test
    6. Improve

    Every problem can be approached as a system.

  2. 2019founded

    Colart Digital Marketing Agency

    Founder & Agency Lead

    Colart started as an entrepreneurial step taken alongside his work in education. It grew from a simple observation: businesses need connected solutions, not isolated services. Branding, design, content, social media, websites and strategy, built to work together as one ecosystem.

  3. 2019

    Judge, World Robot Olympiad

    WRO

    From assisting students to evaluating them. Judging competitive robotics demanded close observation, fairness and technical understanding inside a structured competition.

    Moving from participation to responsibility.

  4. 2020to 2022

    Robotics Instructor & Administrative Assistant

    3Poli Robotics · Genius House Tripoli

    As an instructor he guided students to experiment, build, fail, adjust and try again. At the same time, administration at Genius House taught him scheduling, coordination, documentation and the structure behind daily operations.

    Knowing something and being able to explain it are two different skills.

  5. 2023Mar to Oct

    Executive Manager

    Genius House, Ardeh

    Responsible for daily management, staff, scheduling, educational programs and problem-solving, working with employees, students, parents and management to keep everything running.

    Leadership is more than giving instructions.

  6. 202311 May

    Mentor, Elite World Record

    3Poli Robotics & Genius House

    Samuel mentored at the Elite World Records attempt for the most robotic models assembled simultaneously in a single location. The count reached 300, and the record was set by 3Poli Robotics and Genius House.

    300 robots, one room, one record.

  7. 2023to 2024

    Telemarketing Sales

    Elite Club UAE

    No visuals, little time and a stranger on the line. Outbound sales taught him to find what matters to a customer, handle objections, follow up and communicate value clearly. He now brings those lessons to every Colart proposal.

    Good sales is not talking more. It's listening better.

  8. 2024to 2025

    Bartender

    Tino's Karaoke Pub

    Hospitality is instant feedback. Behind the bar he saw how atmosphere, speed and small details shape what customers feel, and how a business is experienced through every single interaction.

    A brand is not only its logo. It's how people are treated.

  9. 2025to present

    Karaoke Host

    Tune Karaoke by Tino's

    A host sets the rhythm of the night: reading the room, encouraging performers, managing transitions and turning single performances into a shared experience. Live audiences respond, or don't, in real time.

    Attention, energy and timing, without a second take.

  10. Today

    Still being written

    Leading Colart, developing new digital products and services, and hosting at Tune Karaoke by Tino's, exploring where creativity, technology, marketing and human connection meet.

Strategy before noise

Being visible matters. Being memorable is harder. Being relevant matters most.

Start with the business

Before any design, content or campaign, Samuel asks the questions that decide what's actually needed.

  • Who is the customer?
  • Why should they choose you?
  • What does your presence say today?
  • Where are the gaps?

A brand is an experience

A logo, typography and colors are only part of it. A brand is also its tone of voice, its service, its pricing, its response time and its people. Every interaction shapes perception, so what a brand promises must match what customers actually live.

Creativity that works

He cares how something looks and how it works: not only how a campaign earns attention, but what happens after someone clicks. Design should have a purpose, marketing an objective, and technology should solve a real problem.
